---
title: Questionnaire
description: Questionnaire asks questions one step at a time, in a conversation or a panel.
isNew: true
section: AI
---

A `__header` with the title of the set, a `__progress` bar, the `__item` being asked (a `__title`, a `__description`, the choices, an `__error`) and the `__actions` that move between steps. The choices are fields of the library: [cards](/style/components/radio) of radios or checkboxes, or a text field. The share of the steps done is `--questionnaire-progress`, set on the block.
